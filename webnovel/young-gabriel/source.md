# Source — *Young Gabriel: The First Name*

*Being the chronicle of the years before any other Gallant walked, of the boy who listened to the embers, of the things he met and the things he named, and of the morning entropy was given its true name and could not bear it.*

> *And in the beginning there was a Word, and the Word was a fire, and the fire was a quiet thing, and the quiet thing had a face, and the face was a child's.*
> — fragment, unattributed, recovered later from a stone

> *To name a thing is to invite it to be true.*
> — what the embers said, every night of his life, until they did not need to

> *He arrived on time. He always arrived on time. The trouble was that on time was sometimes a long way away.*
> — of the Walking that came after
