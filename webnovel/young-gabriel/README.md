# Young Gabriel — The First Name

> *To name a thing is to invite it to be true.*

A web novel adaptation of the prose chronicle *Young Gabriel*, restructured into bite-sized chapters and clearly-defined arcs in the modern light-novel / cultivation-flavored webnovel style. Designed to be serialized on platforms like WebNovel, RoyalRoad, Tapas, Wattpad, and ScribbleHub.

---

## The Pitch

In a young world where words have not all been spoken, a boy is born beneath a tree that hasn't yet decided what kind of tree it is. He looks at it, opens his mouth, and says one word — **"Fig."**

And the tree agrees.

He is **Gabriel**, though he will not know his own name for twenty-three more years. He is the **Namer**, the one whose mouth the embers of every hearth in the world have been waiting for. Every name he speaks becomes true. Every thing he speaks becomes itself.

But there is something in the north that has never been named — a place that has been hosting an eater of worlds for longer than language has existed, because the place was lonely and could not lock its door. The embers cannot tell Gabriel its name. He will have to look at it. He will have to find it. He will have to say it before it eats everything he loves.

His only companions: an impossible sheep, the voices in every fire, and the fifteen children who have not yet been born — the **Gallants** — whose names he carries in his chest like a midwife carries the names of the unborn.

This is the story of the years before the order. This is the chronicle of the **First Name**.

- **Vibe tags:** `#WordMagic` `#TrueNames` `#WiseMC` `#AnimalCompanion` `#OldGods` `#NoRomance(early)` `#SlowBurn` `#MythicTone` `#WeeklySerial`

## Audience

Readers who loved:
- *The Name of the Wind* (Rothfuss) — for the lyrical voice and the magic of speaking truly
- *Earthsea* (Le Guin) — for true-name magic and the moral weight of speech
- *The Stormlight Archive* (Sanderson) — for the long apprenticeship and the order forming
- *Mushishi* — for the quiet, episodic encounters with spirit-beings
- *Frieren* — for the bittersweet long-life-short-companion ache
- *Vinland Saga* (saga arc) — for the patient, season-by-season pacing
