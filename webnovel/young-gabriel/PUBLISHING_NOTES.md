# Publishing Notes — *Young Gabriel*

How to take this from manuscript to popular webnovel.

---

## Platform Strategy

### Tier 1 (post first; widest reach)

- **RoyalRoad** — the right audience for mythic / wise-MC / slow-burn fantasy. The community rewards consistent posting and ranks new books aggressively. *Young Gabriel*'s lyrical prose will stand out here.
- **WebNovel.com** — vast readership; harder to break out, but signing the Spirity Awards or Power-Up program is realistic for this material. Their editors love "wise child" leads.
- **ScribbleHub** — lighter touch, good for early reader-feedback iteration.

### Tier 2 (mirror after week 2)

- **Tapas** — paste in once a week; good for slow-build discovery.
- **Wattpad** — bigger romance audience, but the mythic crowd exists in tags like `#highfantasy` and `#magic`.

### Tier 3 (own-domain / patron)

- **Substack** with a chapter-a-week newsletter. Pin Chapter 1 as a permanent landing page.
- **Patreon** with $3 / $5 / $10 tiers offering 2 / 4 / 6 chapters ahead. Standard for the genre.

---

## Posting Schedule

**Recommended:** 2 chapters/week. Tuesday + Friday. 6 PM US Eastern (peak global window).

- **Burn rate:** Write 8 chapters before you start posting. Never let your buffer drop below 4. Burnout kills webnovels more than bad writing.
- **Posting window:** Aim to finish Book One in ~30 weeks. Take a 2-week break before Book Two.
- **Hiatus rule:** Never go more than 7 days without a post or an announcement. Readers leave.

---

## Chapter Hooks

The first three chapters do all the heavy lifting. Optimize them ruthlessly.

### Chapter 1 — A Quiet Birth
**Goal:** Establish the magic system in image, not exposition. The mother's labor, the stopped wind, the still sea, the light that leans in to cup her face. End on: *Fig.* and the woman's dropped bowl.

**The first line is everything.** Suggested: *She bore him by a fig tree.* (As in the source. Don't fix what isn't broken.)

### Chapter 2 — She Who
**Goal:** Show the watching infant. The embers begin to speak. End on the boy opening his mouth.

### Chapter 3 — Coal
**Goal:** Three namings in one chapter — *Coal*, *Bowl*, *She Who*. The genre-promise is now visible. End on the mother whispering *Child* into the top of his head, and the boy saying *Yes.*

**If a reader has read these three and not subscribed, they were never going to.**

Avoid: `Harem`, `OP MC`, `Reincarnation`, `Cultivation`, `System`. Wrong audience.

## Marketing Beats

### Week 1
- Post Chapters 1–3.
- Cross-post to r/fantasywriters, r/WeeklyFiction, r/redditserials.
- Post a "I just started this" thread on the RoyalRoad New Releases forum.

### Week 4 (8 chapters in)
- First review push. Ask 3 readers personally for honest reviews. Do not buy reviews.
- Submit to ImmersionAndDiscord servers (Royal Road specific: Sebastian Carrillo, Aiomidoro, FrayedTales).

### Week 8 (16 chapters in — Arc 1 complete)
- Make Chapter 8 a "soft finale." Hint at the wider world. End on the boy stepping onto the first road.
- Run a 24-hour push: announce on Twitter/Bluesky, link from Substack, ask early readers to share.

### Week 12 (Arc 2 finale — Vesta named)
- This is your first viral-friendly chapter. The image of the unnamed woman weeping by an unmade fire is shareable. Make a quote-card.

### Week 24 (Antrum / Maliphant arc)
- Stagger the climax across **three** chapters instead of one. The naming, the falling-out, the brother. Readers will binge it. Pin a "what to read next" comment that previews Chapter 59 (*Gabriel*).

### Week 30 (Book One complete)
- Compile to KDP and Smashwords as a paid eBook.
- Free first 5 chapters as a permanent funnel.
- Begin Book Two announcement post.

## Reader-Retention Tactics

1. **Eshe is the audience surrogate.** Every chapter, give her at least one beat — a head against a knee, a refusal to enter a place, a small bleat at a stranger. Readers will love her more than they love Gabriel. *That is fine.* It pulls them through the slow chapters.

2. **Reveal the Naming rules slowly.** By Chapter 10, the reader should know that Gabriel must *look* before he names. By Chapter 20, that a name can be refused. By Chapter 40, that he cannot name himself. By Chapter 56, that he cannot name what he has not been able to feel grief for. Each rule should arrive *as a consequence*, not as exposition.

3. **Promise the order.** Mention the Gallants — by name, in dreams, in the embers' whispers — from Chapter 12 onward. Readers love a "the order is coming" promise. Pay it off in Chapter 62.

4. **The mother is the emotional load-bearing wall.** Return to her three times. Never tell us how she's feeling; show her hands. The third return — the burial — should be a stand-alone chapter that lands in the middle of Arc 4 as a flashback ("Years later, when he buried her..."). It will get the most comments of any chapter.

5. **Vesta returns.** She is the warmest character in the book. Bring her back briefly in the False-Kings arc (she saves Gabriel at the cult temple in Chapter 23) and again as a quiet ember in Chapter 58.

## Common Webnovel Mistakes to Avoid

- **Don't make Gabriel "OP."** He is bound by attention, by kindness, and by the limits of what the embers have told him. He cannot name what he has not seen. He cannot force a naming on someone who is not the thing he calls them. Resist the urge to give him a power that "shorts the system." The story is about *moral weight*, not power.
- **Don't add a romance for Gabriel.** He is not yet a person. Romance in Book One would feel hollow. Save it. If pressed, Godfrey can have a love-interest in Book Two.
- **Don't kill Eshe early for cheap emotion.** Her death is the emotional spine of Book Three, after Maliphant. Don't spend it.
- **Don't explain Maliphant before Chapter 50.** The villain works because the reader has been told the *shape* of him for fifty chapters and is given the *face* only at the end.
- **Don't capitalize the embers.** Readers will pick up the convention if you're consistent.

## Cover Art Direction

A single-image cover that reads at thumbnail size. Recommended composition:

- A small boy, back to camera, walking onto a flat plain.
- A single sheep at his hip.
- A horizon of low fire — embers across the whole bottom third.
- Title in serif: **YOUNG GABRIEL** / subtitle small: *The First Name*
- Subtle color: warm orange/sienna in the lower third, dust-grey above. No blue, no purple. Mythic, not magical.

Avoid: glowing eyes, swords, runes floating in the air, anime style. The cover should tell readers this is *Earthsea-shaped*, not *cultivation-shaped*.
